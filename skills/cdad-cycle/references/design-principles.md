# Design principles

Principles that govern how the CDAD roles reason about the design of the
product. Each principle has **one** canonical house in this tree: the roles
that owe it point here and never restate its wording.

## General before specific

**The principle.** When a rule can be stated at two levels — a general one
that covers the whole family of cases, and a specific one that covers only
the case at hand — prefer the general one, and narrow it with a stated
exception when the case really needs it.

**The limit.** The principle is a rule of judgement, not a quota: the principle
improves design judgement; it does not increase the justifications that agents
must produce. A decision that is right for the case at hand, with its reason
written down, is enough — the principle never obliges anyone to build an
abstraction that the real use does not need, and it is never an extra
paragraph to fill in.

**Scope.** This file owns the wording of the principle and of its limit. The
architect and the reviewer state their own duty in their own role source and
point to this file; this file does not restate their duties.
